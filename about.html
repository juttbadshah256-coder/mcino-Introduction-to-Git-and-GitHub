About Us

Welcome to the Simple Interest Calculator website. This website is designed to provide a simple and easy way to calculate simple interest.

Our Website

The Simple Interest Calculator allows users to calculate simple interest by entering the principal amount, rate of interest, and time period.

Our Purpose

The purpose of this website is to make simple interest calculations quick, easy, and understandable for everyone.

How It Works

Users provide three basic values: the principal amount, the interest rate, and the time period. The calculator then calculates the simple interest using the standard simple interest formula.

Simple Interest Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100
